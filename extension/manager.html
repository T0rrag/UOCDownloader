    What is included in the ZIP?

    Available attachments are selected by default and keep their original format. Source-page HTML and online bookmarks are optional and unchecked by default. Publication notices are shown as “Not available”, not saved as statement files. Forums are excluded. Source-page links still point online. Decorative images are not treated as assignment files. Direct linked PDF, ZIP, document and media URLs can be included; external tool pages remain online references.

    Each activity module retains its full title, a PEC / PR / Reto folder with 0. Enunciado, 1. Trabajo and 2. Entrega, plus a sibling learning-resources folder. General resources use 0 - Recursos de Aprendizaje de la Asignatura. A JSON report records unavailable items, detected dates, scan errors and original links.

    1. Trabajo and 2. Entrega are empty directory entries. This extension never reads your local work or submission files. Every export includes all selected files, even if you downloaded them before. Extracting the ZIP is a separate action; check your extractor’s overwrite prompt when merging with existing folders.

    Passwords, cookies and tokens are not read or exported. Only preferences, destination overrides and the last report are stored locally. File bytes are held temporarily while the archive is built. No upload or submission requests are made.

    Default permissions cover only aula.uoc.edu. Optional HTTPS file-host permissions may be requested after a scan; granting one does not grant access to every website. Direct file-host requests omit credentials. Live access still depends on UOC and your account.

    Unofficial personal utility · Not affiliated with UOC or Instructure · Original materials stay in your browser until saved to your computer.
